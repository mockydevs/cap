Cap

Record your screen, or record a meeting and get the notes in the Calari portal.

What it does

  • Screen recording. Record your screen, a window or a region, with your voice.
  • Meeting notes. Record a meeting as audio or video. Cap writes the transcript and sends it to the right client in Calari.
  • Stops on its own. When the call ends, the window closes, or no one has spoken for a long time (it warns you first).

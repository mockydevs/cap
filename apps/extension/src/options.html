Cap options

Microphone

A meeting tab carries the other people's voices, not yours. Allow the microphone so your side is recorded too.

Default
Google Calendar

In a Google Calendar event, choose Include Cap (or type [Cap: record audio] or [Cap: record video] in the description). Guests can see that line, so everyone knows the meeting will be recorded. When the meeting starts, Cap reminds you and you start it with one click: Chrome does not let extensions start recording a tab on their own.
